# Onshape for FTC

In this course you learn to model robot parts in Onshape, a free CAD that runs in the browser. By the end, each module team (Shooter, Turret, Intake + Transfer, Chassis) builds its module as an assembly of goBILDA parts, and we order parts from that model.

## Plan

| # | Meeting (1.5 h) | Homework |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sketch, constraints, extrude, holes, pattern | 6 parts from drawings |
| 2 | goBILDA parts, assemblies, mates | A small shaft + bearing + gear unit |
| 3 | Master sketch, variables, your module | Draft of your module for design review |

## Before the first meeting

- [ ] Laptop with Chrome or Edge (Onshape also has an iPad app, but a laptop is much easier).
- [ ] A mouse with a scroll wheel. A touchpad works, but slower.
- [ ] An Onshape account: go to [onshape.com/education](https://www.onshape.com/en/education), choose **Student**, and sign up with your school email. It's free.

## Rules

- Every homework part goes in its own Onshape document named `Lastname - T25 - Grid plate` and so on.
- You check your own model before submitting: every drawing comes with the **volume** your part must have.
- Stuck for more than 15 minutes? Ask in the team chat with a screenshot. Asking is faster than guessing.
